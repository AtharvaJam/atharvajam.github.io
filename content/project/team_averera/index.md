## Project Objective

> The objective of this project was to train a visual imitation learning agent for a robotic pouring task, and validate the performance in both simulated and real environments.

#### Contributors

The project was performed as part of a student research team of Indian Institute of Technology (B.H.U.), Varanasi. The team members can be found [here](https://www.teamaverera.com/our-team-1).
